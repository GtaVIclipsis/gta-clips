Privacy Policy

GTA Clip Uploader • Effective Date: November 21, 2026 • Last Updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA Clip Uploader ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit our website (https://gtaviclipsis.github.io/gta-clips/) or authorize our application to connect with your TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram creator accounts.

1. Data Controller & Contact Information

The operator responsible for data processing within GTA Clip Uploader can be reached at:

Application: GTA Clip Uploader
Website: https://gtaviclipsis.github.io/gta-clips/
Privacy & Support Email: sensoren.lieber.5d@icloud.com

2. Information We Collect and Process

We collect only the minimum information strictly necessary to authenticate your authorized creator account and publish videos at your explicit direction:

  • TikTok Account & Profile Data (user.info.basic): When you authorize GTA Clip Uploader via TikTok OAuth 2.0, we receive your TikTok open_id, display name, and basic profile information required to identify your connected creator channel and query posting eligibility via /v2/post/publish/creator_info/query/.
  • OAuth 2.0 Credentials: Access tokens, refresh tokens, granted permission scopes, and token expiration timestamps issued by TikTok (and, when enabled by you, Google/YouTube or Meta/Instagram) so uploads and status checks can be executed on your behalf.
  • Video Files & Publishing Metadata (video.upload & video.publish): MP4 video files, video titles, captions, hashtags, selected privacy levels (PUBLIC_TO_EVERYONE, MUTUAL_FOLLOW_FRIENDS, FOLLOWER_OF_CREATOR, or SELF_ONLY), interaction toggles (comment, duet, stitch), commercial content disclosure flags, and resulting platform publish_id/video IDs.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Logs: Basic error and upload-status logs (such as HTTP status codes and publish-status responses) used solely to verify delivery and troubleshoot failed uploads.
  • No Advertising Trackers or Invasive Cookies: Our website does not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or behavioral profiling scripts.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information strictly to provide the requested creator publishing functionality:

  • To authenticate your creator session using official OAuth 2.0 flows.
  • To retrieve creator posting constraints (available privacy levels, maximum video duration, and interaction settings) before initiating a Direct Post.
  • To format, preview, upload, and publish short-form gaming videos according to your chosen settings.
  • To monitor upload completion status and prevent duplicate publishing of the same clip.
  • To respond to technical support, data access, or deletion requests sent to sensoren.lieber.5d@icloud.com.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA, UK, and International Users)

Where applicable under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws, we rely on:

  • Consent: Your explicit OAuth 2.0 authorization granting specific scopes and your explicit confirmation of publishing settings prior to posting.
  • Performance of a Service: Processing video files and metadata necessary to fulfill your requested upload and publishing workflow.
  • Legitimate Interests: Maintaining system security, preventing duplicate posts, and diagnosing technical API errors.

5. Third-Party Platform APIs & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal data or video content to any third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.

Data is transmitted solely to the official platform APIs you explicitly authorize:

  • TikTok API Services: Video files, captions, and publishing parameters are sent directly to TikTok's Content Posting API v2 in accordance with the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube API Services (Optional): Where YouTube publishing is enabled by the creator, uploads interact with the YouTube Data API v3 in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Storage, Retention & Security

  • Transport & Credential Security: All API communications use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. OAuth tokens and client secrets are stored in restricted-access environments and are never exposed in client-side browser code.
  • Media Retention: Temporary processed video files and staging assets are automatically purged within 72 hours after upload completion (or immediately upon manual deletion).
  • Token Retention: OAuth tokens are retained only while your account remains connected. Revoking permissions or requesting deletion removes associated tokens immediately.

7. Your Rights, Access Revocation & Data Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR in the EU/EEA/UK and CCPA/CPRA in California), you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent at any time.

  • Revoke TikTok Access: Open the TikTok app or website → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → select GTA Clip Uploader → tap Remove access.
  • Revoke Google/YouTube Access: Visit your Google Account Permissions page and remove GTA Clip Uploader.
  • Request Full Data Deletion: Email sensoren.lieber.5d@icloud.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will verify and permanently delete any retained tokens, logs, and metadata within 48 hours.

8. Children's Privacy

GTA Clip Uploader is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where a higher minimum age applies). We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided information through our service, please contact sensoren.lieber.5d@icloud.com for immediate removal.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to GTA Clip Uploader or changes in platform API requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
