Frequently Asked Questions & Support

Key details about permissions, Direct Post compliance, and creator account control.

Which TikTok permissions does GTA Clip Uploader use?

GTA Clip Uploader requests user.info.basic (to identify your connected creator profile), video.upload (to transfer video chunks securely), and video.publish (to submit direct posts with your chosen privacy and interaction settings).

Can I disconnect my TikTok or YouTube account at any time?

Yes. You can revoke access anytime from your TikTok App Permissions settings or Google Account Security settings. You can also request complete deletion of any stored metadata by emailing sensoren.lieber.5d@icloud.com.
